Who we are

A technical solutions company, not just a product supplier.

NESIAK is a solutions company for the industrial sector, focused especially on the Oil & Gas market, supplying equipment, components, technical solutions and specialized services.

We were born from the combination of engineering, experience, technology and field knowledge. More than supplying equipment, we seek to understand the client's needs, assess the technical requirements of the application and develop a solution that contributes to a safer, more reliable and more efficient operation.

Inspired by Kaizen
“There is always a better way to do it.”

Our identity is inspired by the Japanese concept of Kaizen, associated with continuous improvement. For NESIAK, this means learning from every project, every piece of equipment, every problem and every experience, and using that knowledge to develop ever better solutions.

Inspection and technical services

Compliance, quality, documentation and traceability.

Beyond supply, we provide technical support to verify that every piece of equipment and component meets its specification.

Receiving inspection

Assurance that incoming material matches what was purchased.

  • Dimensional and visual check
  • Identification and marking check
  • Certificate and documentation review
  • Quantity and material condition check
  • Traceability check

Manufacturing inspection

Follow-up of each stage so the final product meets the drawing and specification.

  • Manufacturing follow-up
  • Dimensional and visual inspection
  • Material and component verification
  • Manufacturing stage control
  • Drawing and specification requirement check
  • Final inspection and release

Inspection of Oil & Gas tubulars and components

Critical production components inspected with technical rigor.

  • Tubing and casing
  • Couplings
  • Nipples and pup joints
  • Crossovers
  • Connections
  • Pump components
  • Production equipment components

Valve and equipment inspection

Full verification before the equipment reaches the field.

  • Visual and dimensional inspection
  • Identification and traceability
  • Documentation review
  • Test witnessing
  • Purchase requirement check
  • Pre-delivery inspection

Supplier inspection

We provide technical follow-up of suppliers, representing the client's interests during equipment manufacturing and inspection.

  • Manufacturing follow-up
  • Test witnessing
  • Critical stage inspection
  • Final inspection
  • Documentation review
  • Technical report issuance

Testing and special inspections

Non-destructive testing and inspections to confirm material, integrity and dimensions.

PMIPositive Material IdentificationUTUltrasonic TestingMTMagnetic Particle TestingPTLiquid Penetrant TestingVTVisual Testing
  • Thickness measurement
  • Dimensional inspections

Tests that require specific personnel, procedures or qualifications are performed by NESIAK only when the applicable structure and qualifications are in place, or by duly qualified professionals and companies.

Quality control and documentation

All the documentation that proves the quality and traceability of each supply.

  • Traceability
  • Inspection and Test Plans (ITP)
  • Inspection reports
